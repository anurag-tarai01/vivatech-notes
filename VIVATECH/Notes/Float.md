> **The digital balance an agent (or system) holds to perform transactions.**

---

## 💡 Simple Meaning

Float = **working money available to operate**

It’s not profit. It’s not savings.  
It’s the **money you need in hand (digitally) to serve customers.**

---

## 🧠 In Your System (Agent Perspective)

An agent deals with two things:

- Physical cash (in shop)
- Digital balance (in wallet)

👉 **Float = digital balance in agent’s wallet**

---

## 🔁 How Float Works

### Example: Cash-In

Customer gives ₹1000 cash to agent

- Agent gives ₹1000 **digital money**
- Agent’s wallet **decreases**
- Agent’s physical cash **increases**

➡️ Agent **used float**

---

### Example: Cash-Out

Customer wants ₹1000 cash

- Agent gives ₹1000 **physical cash**
- Agent receives ₹1000 **digital money**
- Agent’s wallet **increases**

➡️ Agent **gains float**

---

## ⚖️ Key Insight

Float is always moving:

- Cash-in → float **goes down**
- Cash-out → float **goes up**

---

## 🚨 Why Float is Critical

If agent has:

- ❌ No float → cannot do **cash-in**
- ❌ No cash → cannot do **cash-out**

So agents must **balance both**:

- Physical cash
- Digital float

---

## 🏦 System-Level Float

There is also **company float (e.g., AMT01 wallet)**:

- Used to **recharge agent wallets**
- Controlled by admins
- Keeps the whole network running

---

## ⚡ One-Line Definition

**Float = the digital balance available to execute transactions.**

---

## 🔗 Quick Relation

- **Liquidity** → ability to use money quickly
- **Float** → actual usable digital balance
