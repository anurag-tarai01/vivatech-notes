> To send money out

### 🧠 Real-life example:

- Company pays salary → disburse
- Bank sends loan → disburse

---

### 🏦 In YOUR system:

Disburse = money going **OUT to external network**

---

### Example (Switch case):

Subscriber sends money to MTN:

Subscriber → Switch Wallet → External (MTN)

👉 That external send = **DISBURSE**

---

### In code:

SwitchWalletService.sendMoney()

👉 This is disbursement call

---

### Opposite of disburse?

👉 **Collect / Receive**
