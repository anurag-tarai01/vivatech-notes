> How much money is available right now to use

### Real-life example (India):

- You have ₹10,000 in bank → **high liquidity**
- You have ₹10,000 in FD → **low liquidity** (can’t use instantly)

## Everyday Examples

- **Cash in your pocket** → 100% liquid (you can use it instantly)
- **Money in bank account** → highly liquid (can withdraw anytime)
- **Gold, land, property** → low liquidity (need time to sell)

Liquidity = how quickly and easily money can be used when needed.

---
### In YOUR system:

Liquidity = money available in wallets to process transactions

#### Example:

- Switch Wallet = 50,000  
    That means:

> System can send up to 50,000 via switch
